      Field notes

      • WeatherNovember runs cool, wet and windy. Bring a windproof layer and shoes with grip for Lands End's stairs.
      • DaylightSunset is just before 5 PM all week. Plan the coast and the redwoods for the morning and early afternoon.
      • Muir WoodsParking reservation required, and there's no cell signal. Download the receipt and trail map before you go.
      • Rental carAvis, 500 Beach St. Nothing visible in the car at overlooks. The southbound bridge toll gets billed through the rental.
      • Book nowMuir Woods parking, the rental car, Exploratorium After Dark (Thu) and the City Lights Cruise (Fri). Conference and training run Mon–Fri, so sightseeing is the weekend and evenings.
